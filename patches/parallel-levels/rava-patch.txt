# MinecraftServer.tickChildren ticks each level in turn: fire LevelTickEvent.Pre, tick the
# level, fire LevelTickEvent.Post. The hooks queue the level instead, and the next profiler
# section switch after the loop runs the queued levels: every Pre event, the level ticks in
# parallel, every Post event.
